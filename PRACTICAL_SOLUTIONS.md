## 🔧 **Solution 3: Third-Party Aggregators (Actually Work)**

### **1. Yodlee (Now Envestnet) - ✅ WORKS**
- **URL:** https://developer.yodlee.com/
- **Reality:** Provides real bank data
- **Approval:** Easier than individual banks
- **Cost:** $0.50-$2.00 per API call
- **Coverage:** 100+ banks including Indian banks
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for approval

### **2. Perfios - ✅ WORKS**
- **URL:** https://www.perfios.com/
- **Reality:** Indian bank focus
- **Approval:** Business use case required
- **Cost:** ₹50,000+ per month
- **Coverage:** Major Indian banks
- **Timeline:** 2-3 weeks for approval

### **3. RazorpayX - ✅ WORKS**
- **URL:** https://razorpay.com/x/
- **Reality:** Developer-friendly
- **Approval:** Easier for fintech startups
- **Cost:** Transaction-based pricing
- **Coverage:** Limited but growing
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for approval
